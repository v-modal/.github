## 💡 Why V-Modal

Video is the fastest-growing data type on the planet — and the least searchable. Cameras, phones, smart glasses and physical-AI devices produce hours of footage that today can only be found by filename or hand-written tags.

V-Modal analyzes the actual frames and context of your videos, so people and machines can retrieve any moment by **meaning** — through natural language, an image, or another video. We ship it as small, typed SDKs that drop into the apps and devices developers already build.

## ⚡ Key capabilities

* **Natural-language video search** — analyzes actual video frames and contextual data, so users search video content with conversational phrases.
* **Multimodal queries** — text-to-video, image-to-video and video-to-video search intents through one API.
* **Cross-platform SDKs** — a native [Android Kotlin SDK](https://github.com/v-modal/vmodal_sdk_android) and a cross-platform [Flutter SDK](https://github.com/v-modal/vmodal_sdk_flutter) with a uniform, typed surface.
* **Built for mobile media** — signed streaming URLs, chunked multipart uploads, progress and real cancellation, so massive video archives never crash or slow down devices.

## 🎯 Who builds on V-Modal

* **Media & entertainment apps** — let viewers jump to the precise scene inside a massive video catalog.
* **E-commerce & social commerce** — visual search that surfaces product clips from an image or a description.
* **Surveillance & security** — scan hours of security or dashcam footage for one specific visual indicator.
* **Smart glasses & physical AI** — give devices a visual memory they can query.
